# Product

## Name

Masarak (مسارك) — “from your result to a closer choice.”

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Egyptian Thanaweya Amma students are the primary audience, typically using a mobile phone at a stressful, time-sensitive moment after results are published. Parents are a secondary audience helping students understand the result and realistic university options.

## Product Purpose

The product gives students two focused tools: private result lookup by seat number or Arabic name, and deterministic faculty predictions based on score distributions, relative rank, historical cutoffs, and an optional governorate-based proximity order. Success means a student can find a result quickly, understand it, transfer it into prediction, and leave with a clear range of nearby and nationwide options without mistaking an estimate for an official decision.

## Positioning

A fast, transparent bridge from a Thanaweya Amma result to realistic faculty options, grounded in comparable ranking data rather than misleading raw-score comparisons.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and precise. The voice should reduce anxiety, explain uncertainty plainly, and feel familiar to Egyptian students and parents without imitating a government service.

## Anti-references

The product must not resemble an outdated government portal, a sensational result-news site, a commercial tutoring marketplace, or an advertisement-led ranking page. Avoid fake official seals, crowded navigation, intrusive ads, exaggerated guarantees, decorative gradients, and generic stock photography.

## Design Principles

- Put the student’s task above the platform’s content: the two tools are immediately available.
- Use governorate only to reorder and filter options; never present proximity as an official geographic-distribution decision.
- Lead with realistic target and safe options; keep reach options secondary and hide unlikely options behind an explicit disclosure.
- Explain uncertainty at the exact point where it affects a decision.
- Make every number comparable and traceable to a year, scale, or rank basis.
- Protect personal result data by default, not as an afterthought.
- Keep the experience calm under load: familiar controls, short forms, fast responses, and clear recovery states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic RTL is the default. The interface targets WCAG 2.2 AA, supports keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion, high-contrast status communication that does not rely on color alone, readable Arabic numerals and Latin digits inside RTL text, and responsive layouts from 360px upward.
